Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 3, 2026

Thank you for using Basith Proxy (“the App”). Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles information when you use it.

Information Collection

Basith Proxy is a local network debugging and proxy tool designed for developers to inspect and analyze network traffic.

The App does not collect, store, transmit, or share personal information with external servers.

All network traffic captured through the App remains locally on your device unless you explicitly choose to export or share data yourself.

Network Traffic Inspection

When enabled by the user, the App may inspect HTTP and HTTPS traffic routed through the local proxy configured on the device.

This may include:

  • Request URLs
  • Headers
  • Response data
  • Cookies
  • API payloads

This information is processed locally on your device solely for debugging and development purposes.

HTTPS / SSL Inspection

To inspect encrypted HTTPS traffic, users may choose to install and trust a locally generated SSL certificate created by the App.

The certificate:

  • Is generated locally on the user’s device
  • Is not uploaded to any external server
  • Is used only for traffic inspection on the user’s device

Users are fully responsible for enabling or disabling SSL inspection.

Data Storage

Captured session data may be stored locally on your device to allow session history and debugging workflows.

The App does not automatically upload any captured data to cloud services or third parties.

Third-Party Services

Basith Proxy does not include:

  • Third-party analytics
  • Advertising SDKs
  • Tracking frameworks
  • Remote telemetry services

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties.

Security

We take reasonable measures to ensure the App operates securely. However, users should avoid inspecting sensitive traffic on untrusted networks and should manage exported session files carefully.

Children’s Privacy

Basith Proxy is intended for developers and technical users and is not directed toward children under the age of 13.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the effective date above.
